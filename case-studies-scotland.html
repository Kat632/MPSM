Inside the Schoolseum Project

A museum as a place for learning

Schoolseum was developed at V&A Dundee, Scotland's first dedicated design museum. The project drew on Wendy James' My Primary School is at the Museum concept and explored how cultural settings can support learning, creativity and career awareness.

The school partner was Rowantree Primary School in Dundee, described in the report as the highest-ranked school in the Scottish Index of Multiple Deprivation (SIMD).

Exploring creative disciplines

Pupils worked across four areas: jewellery and fashion design, architecture and engineering, game design, and curation and exhibition design. These activities offered opportunities to explore ideas, experiment, solve problems and communicate their thinking.

Learning beyond the museum

The project also included visits to other Dundee cultural and educational organisations:

  • McManus Galleries
  • RSS Discovery
  • HMS Unicorn
  • Dundee Science Centre

The project concluded with a showcase day for families, educators and the wider community. The report noted that many families had not visited a museum before.

Learning and Impact

What Did the Evaluation Find?

Assessing pupils' development

Student self-assessments used the Stellar Assessment Tool to examine areas including research, fixing and failing, reflection, concept development and communication.

Measured improvements

  • 85% of students improved in at least two assessed areas.
  • 82% increased their overall score.
  • Communication scores rose by 519 points, representing an average increase of 16%.

Individual progress

The report highlighted Student B as the largest individual improvement, with a score increasing from 20 to 89.

Looking ahead

Teachers observed increased curiosity among pupils. The project also attracted interest from Education Scotland, with efforts made to secure funding for future development.

These findings offer an encouraging example of how creative, museum-based learning can support children's development and connect classroom learning with cultural experiences.
