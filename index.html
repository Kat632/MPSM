How We Do It

MPSM is dedicated to bridging the gap between formal education and the rich resources of museums. Our work is built upon four key pillars, driving innovation and impact in primary education.

Partnerships

Facilitating strong, sustainable partnerships between primary schools and museums to create enriching, fully inclusive educational experiences.

Read more
Research

Conducting research to understand the impact of museum-based learning on primary school children, informing best practices and policy.

International Collaboration

Working with global partners to share insights, develop new methodologies, and expand the reach of museum-school initiatives worldwide.
